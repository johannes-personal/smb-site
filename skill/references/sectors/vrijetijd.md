# Sector pack: vrijetijd / dagje uit

Attractions, play parks, mazes, petting zoos, mini-golf, indoor playgrounds,
campsites with day activities.

## What the visitor is deciding
"Shall we go there today, or this weekend, with these children, in this
weather?" It is a fast decision made on a phone, often that same morning, and it
hinges on four things: **is it open, what does it cost, is it suitable for my
kids' ages, and what happens if it rains.**

## Facts that matter here
- **Opening hours are the whole ballgame** and they are seasonal, weather-
  dependent and keyed to school holidays. This is the sector where the hours
  rule engine earns its keep. A "next open" line near the top is worth more
  than any hero copy
- Prices, with age tiers and what is included. Free-under-X is a real decision
  factor for parents
- Season window — when does the season start and end, and does the headline
  attraction depend on it (a maize maze does not exist in May)
- Suitability: age range, indoor/outdoor, accessibility for buggies
- Parking and cost of parking
- Whether you may bring your own food — surprisingly decisive for families

## Page structure
Homepage sections, in order:
1. `Hero` — one strong photo of children actually enjoying the place, the
   promise in one line, primary action
2. `HoursSummary` — next open, immediately. Not buried on a hours page
3. `QuickLinks` — the four things people came for
4. `FeatureRow` — the headline attraction, explained properly
5. `CardGrid` — what else there is to do
6. `PriceTable` — referencing facts
7. `MapContact` — where, parking, route
8. `Gallery` — proof it is a real place, with honest captions
9. `CTABand` — tickets or route

Other pages: the headline attraction, what to do, hours (calendar), prices,
photos, parties and groups, contact, terms.

## Primary action
Usually "buy a ticket" if a ticketing system exists, otherwise "plan a visit"
(route + hours). Repeat it three times. If ticketing is external, embed or link
it rather than rebuilding it.

## Content that earns its place
Weather contingency ("binnen spelen we gewoon door"), age suitability, picnic
policy, free parking, what is new this season.

## Filler to avoid
A long history of the family business above the fold, generic stock photos of
unrelated children, and a list of "faciliteiten" that includes toilets.

## Traps
- Hours photographed on a sign at the entrance and never digitised
- Prices duplicated between the site and the ticketing platform — they drift
- Season-dependent attractions described as if always available
- Photos labelled wrongly; check what each actually shows
- Dated tickets are exempt from the 14-day withdrawal right — see the
  compliance reference before writing terms
