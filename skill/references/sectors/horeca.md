# Sector pack: horeca

Restaurants, cafés, lunchrooms, bakeries, ice cream shops, catering.

## What the visitor is deciding
"Can I eat here tonight, and is it the right kind of place?" Two thirds of this
traffic is someone hungry, on a phone, within a few kilometres. Speed to the
answer matters more than atmosphere on the page — the atmosphere is conveyed by
one good photograph, not by three paragraphs.

## Facts that matter here
- Opening hours, including kitchen hours where they differ from the venue's.
  This distinction is invisible on most restaurant sites and causes real
  irritation
- Menu, with prices and allergen information. Frequently only exists as a PDF
  or a photograph
- Reservation channel — phone, an external booking platform, or walk-in only
- Location, parking, terrace, accessibility
- Dietary options: vegetarian, vegan, gluten-free. Do not overstate these;
  verify with the owner
- Takeaway and delivery, and via which platform

## Page structure
1. `Hero` — one photograph of the food or the room, not a stock image
2. `HoursSummary` — open now / kitchen hours
3. `CTABand` or `Embed` — reserve, via whatever system they already use
4. `FeatureRow` — what kind of place this is, in three sentences
5. `CardGrid` — a few signature dishes or the menu categories
6. `MapContact`
7. `Gallery`

Other pages: menu, reservations, about, contact. Keep it to five.

## Primary action
Reserve, or phone. If they already use a booking platform, embed it — do not
build a reservation system.

## Content that earns its place
The actual menu as text (not only a PDF — a PDF is invisible to search and
painful on a phone), kitchen hours, whether you can walk in, dietary options,
parking.

## Filler to avoid
"Passie voor koken", stock photographs of anonymous plated food, a chef's
biography above the menu, and a gallery of the empty dining room at 3pm.

## Traps
- The menu exists only as a PDF or a Facebook photo. Transcribing it is high
  value and must be confirmed by the owner — allergens especially
- Prices in the PDF and prices on the site diverge. Prefer one source
- Seasonal or weekly-changing menus need a content model that admits change,
  or the site is wrong within a fortnight
- Holiday closures are announced on social media and never on the site
